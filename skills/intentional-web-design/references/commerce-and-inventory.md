# Commerce and inventory

Move from orientation into the real inventory quickly. Put facts beside the item or decision they affect instead of creating decorative summary layers.

## Product and listing collections

For visual goods, properties, services, projects, or comparable inventory:

- Use a stable, scannable grid or list appropriate to comparison.
- Include an intentional image when appearance or setting affects choice.
- Provide a clear title and concise differentiating description.
- Show price or decision-critical metadata prominently when available.
- Provide a clear action leading to a real detail or inquiry path.
- Keep peer geometry stable unless hierarchy is intentional and useful.

Avoid text-only ledgers for visual products and avoid generic collection summaries or decorative statistics that delay the items.

## Business truth

- Never invent availability, scarcity, deadlines, occupancy, pricing, outcomes, or urgency.
- Use supplied live data or omit the claim.
- Place fulfillment, booking, and policy information near the action it changes.

## Footers

- Give the footer one primary closing job: navigation, contact, booking, or trust.
- Group brand description and primary contact with the identity.
- Avoid sparse disconnected columns across an oversized empty field.
- Preserve clear navigation, social access, legal information, and truthful next actions.
